# Community Code of Conduct

Nyctr welcomes people with different backgrounds, abilities, experience levels,
and approaches to building software. Treat contributors and users with respect.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss ideas and technical tradeoffs constructively.
- Give actionable feedback and credit other people's work.
- Respect privacy, boundaries, and differences of experience.
- Accept corrections and help make the project accessible to newcomers.

Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, personal attacks, publishing private
information without consent, and sustained disruption are not acceptable.
These expectations apply to project issues, pull requests, discussions and other
project-controlled community spaces.

## Reporting and enforcement

Contact a repository maintainer privately through a contact method they publish.
If no private method is listed, request one in a minimal issue without including
sensitive details or identifying the affected person. Do not post a report's
private contents in public.

Maintainers may remove content, request corrections, restrict participation or
ban participants in proportion to the behavior and its impact. Reports should
be handled with discretion; maintainers must recuse themselves from a complaint
about their own conduct. Appeals can be directed to another project maintainer
when one is available.
